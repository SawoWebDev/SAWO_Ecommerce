Finishing a Sauna the Right Way

The accessories around a sauna heater are what turn a hot room into an authentic Finnish sauna experience — the pail and ladle for löyly, the backrest that makes a long session comfortable, the lighting that sets the mood. Every accessory here is listed with its real size, capacity and wood finish, so you can match it to the rest of your cabin without guesswork.

Wood finish matters as much as fit. Cedar, Aspen and Hemlock each weather differently in high heat and humidity, and mixing finishes unintentionally is the most common regret we hear from customers finishing a build. If you're matching an existing SAWO room, the finish codes on this page mirror the ones used across our heaters and panelling.

We stock both premium wooden pieces and stainless-steel or metal alternatives, so you can choose based on maintenance and budget without compromising on durability. Every listing includes its product code, so you can cross-reference before you order rather than after.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I match the wood finish to my existing sauna?

Check the finish code on your heater or panelling data plate — Cedar, Aspen and Hemlock are used consistently across our pails, ladles, backrests and doors, so matching the code keeps everything visually consistent.

Can I mix wood finishes in one sauna?

You can, and many customers do for contrast pieces like a Black ladle against Cedar panelling — it's a style choice, not a fit issue, since all finishes are dimensionally identical within a product line.

Are the doors pre-hung or slab only?

Each door listing shows both its opening size and frame size — order against your rough opening, and confirm with our team if you're retrofitting into an existing frame.

How long do wooden accessories last in a sauna?

With regular use and occasional oiling, most wooden pieces last many years. High humidity and mineral buildup from löyly water can dull the finish over time, so periodic light sanding and re-oiling keeps them looking new.

Do you offer a warranty on accessories?

Yes, every accessory sold through SAWO carries a minimum 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects.

Can I return an accessory if the finish doesn't match?

Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 30 days. We'd recommend confirming your finish code with our support team before ordering to avoid the round trip entirely.

Send us your heater or room model and we'll confirm the matching accessories before you order.
